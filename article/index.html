This project turns scattered German radio archive and programme metadata into a corpus for studying biographical radio drama, documentary feature, and sound-based life writing.

It is not a final canon, and the clusters are not evidence on their own. The project is a navigational layer for finding patterns, absences, and promising cases for close listening. It starts from 1,764 merged German records, models the 806 high- and medium-confidence candidates, and displays a 566-record curated map after spreadsheet review and exclusions.

How to read the model

The pipeline is useful for triage, not for settled classification. TF-IDF, truncated SVD, k-means, and t-SNE are used to make a large, uneven archive browsable; the labels are working names checked against top terms, nearest examples, source mix, and manual curation notes. A fuller technical walkthrough is available in the method notebook.

Selection

High confidence means an explicit biographical signal in source metadata or programme text. Medium confidence means weaker but plausible life-writing evidence. Low-confidence and non-life-writing items are held out of the public map rather than treated as negative examples.

Cluster count

The visible map uses six curated spreadsheet clusters for readability. These working labels were assigned after examining terms, examples, source mix, and curation notes.

Source confound

DRA records are often short catalogue descriptions, while DLF and Wirklichkeit pages can be much longer essays. The source-mix views are therefore diagnostic: clusters may reflect register and text length as well as topic.

Validation status

Validation here is interpretive: source cards, protagonist extraction, cluster labels, and exclusions have been human-checked where the map uses them. A formal quality score or independent hand-coded benchmark is still future work.

Corpus triage

These counts describe the 566 visible map records, not the full scrape. DRA supplies the historical backbone; DLF / DLF Kultur adds recent programme pages; Wirklichkeit im Radio is small but text-rich.

Who are these lives about?

For each curated record we extract whom it appears to be about, then add provisional life-focus tags. A record can carry several — the subject line is the anchor, the tags are aids for sorting.
